# 重点反馈

1. `most AI agent / student can` → `most AI agents / students can` → 泛指一类可数事物通常用复数 → 前者已修正，后者仍未稳定。
2. `can based on students background to provide` → `can provide ... based on students' backgrounds` → `can` 后接动词原形，`based on` 作补充信息 → 重写仍说 `can base on`。
3. `a serious of profession materials` → `a series of professional learning materials` → `series` 是“一系列”，名词前用形容词 `professional` → `series` 已修正，`professional` 仍未稳定。
